How it is scored

WPM, raw WPM and accuracy

WPM counts the correct characters left on screen, five to a word, divided by minutes. Raw WPM counts every key you pressed, right or wrong. Accuracy is correct keystrokes over all keystrokes, so a mistake you backspace over still counts: you made it.
